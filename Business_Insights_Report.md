# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics
## Business Insights Report

### 1. Project Overview
This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset using MySQL and Power BI to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, payment methods, and delivery operations.

The objective is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

### 2. Key Business Findings

**1. Overall Sales Performance**
- Total orders: 99,441
- Total revenue: BRL 13,591,643.70
- Total unique customers: 96,096
- Average order value: BRL 137.75
- Average delivery time: 12.50 days

**Insight:** The business generated significant sales revenue across a large customer base. The average order value provides a useful baseline for evaluating future sales growth.

**2. Product Category Performance**

The highest-revenue categories include:
- Health & Beauty
- Watches & Gifts
- Bed, Bath & Table
- Sports & Leisure
- Computers & Accessories

**Insight:** These categories are major revenue contributors and represent important areas for sales and marketing strategies.

**3. Geographic Sales Performance**

The leading states by revenue were:
- São Paulo (SP): BRL 5,202,955.05
- Rio de Janeiro (RJ): BRL 1,824,092.67
- Minas Gerais (MG): BRL 1,585,308.03

**Insight:** São Paulo generated the highest revenue, making it a particularly important market for the business.

**4. Payment Method Analysis**

Credit cards generated the highest revenue:
- Credit Card: BRL 12,542,084.19
- Boleto: BRL 2,869,361.27
- Voucher: BRL 379,436.87
- Debit Card: BRL 217,989.79

**Insight:** Credit cards are the dominant payment method, indicating that customers strongly rely on card-based transactions.

**5. Customer Review Analysis**

- 5-star reviews: 57.78%
- 4-star reviews: 19.29%
- 3-star reviews: 8.24%
- 2-star reviews: 3.18%
- 1-star reviews: 11.51%

**Insight:** Most customers gave positive ratings, but the percentage of 1-star reviews highlights an opportunity to investigate customer dissatisfaction.

**6. Delivery Performance & Customer Satisfaction**

The analysis showed:
- Average review score for on-time deliveries: 4.29
- Average review score for late deliveries: 2.57

**Insight:** Late deliveries are associated with substantially lower customer satisfaction. Improving delivery reliability should be a business priority.

### 3. Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Delivery Operations:** Investigate delivery delays and work with logistics partners to improve on-time delivery performance.
2. **Focus on High-Revenue Categories:** Develop targeted promotions and inventory strategies for the top-performing product categories.
3. **Strengthen Key Markets:** Prioritize marketing and customer acquisition strategies in high-revenue states, particularly São Paulo.
4. **Maintain Payment Convenience:** Continue supporting credit card payments while improving the customer experience across other payment options.
5. **Improve Customer Experience:** Investigate low customer ratings to identify recurring problems involving products, delivery, or service quality.
6. **Increase Average Order Value:** Consider product bundles, cross-selling, and relevant promotional offers to encourage larger purchases.

### 4. Conclusion

This analysis demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be used together to evaluate e-commerce performance and identify actionable business opportunities.

The findings highlight the importance of product category performance, geographic sales distribution, payment preferences, and delivery reliability.

The project provides a practical example of data analysis, SQL querying, data visualization, and business insight generation.
